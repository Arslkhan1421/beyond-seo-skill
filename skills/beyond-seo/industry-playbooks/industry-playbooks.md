## Vertical-specific evidence and implementation

| Vertical | Evidence and content | Structured-data review | Conversion/local requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency | Named team, authorized project proof, scope and limitations | Organization, Service and visible case-study details | Qualified enquiry path; GBP only with real eligible location |
| AI/SaaS | Working product examples, integration docs, privacy/security claims approved by owner | Organization; SoftwareApplication only where applicable | Demo/trial activation and qualified pipeline; never fabricate AI benchmarks |
| Dental | Licensed clinician authors/reviewers, treatment risks and dated clinical sources | Relevant LocalBusiness subtype; visible location, hours and clinician identity | Real GBP practice, appointment flow, accessible contact; health claims require expert review |
| Ecommerce | Product identity, stock, currency, shipping/returns and variation-specific content | Product/Offer prices and availability against visible variant; review authenticity | Checkout and purchase tracking; filter/canonical decisions by actual demand |
| Education | Instructor credentials, syllabus, prerequisites, delivery and refund terms | Course vocabulary where applicable; current Google feature support separately verified | Enrolment completion, clear eligibility and transparent cost |
| Local business | Real service area, address visibility, opening hours and owner-confirmed services | Appropriate LocalBusiness subtype and consistent NAP | GBP eligibility, call/booking actions, no fabricated regional offices |
| Medical | Qualified accountable author/reviewer, evidence dates, patient-safe claims and contact | Actual medical entity vocabulary, never implied certification | Appointment accessibility and consent-safe measurement; clinical review before publication |
| Real estate | Authorized listings, current availability, agent credentials and service geography | Actual listing/entity vocabulary; do not imply unsupported rich results | Genuine enquiry/booking flow, fair housing/legal review by qualified owner |

These are page-purpose checks, not automatic schema prescriptions, legal advice or ranking guarantees. Confirm current official feature requirements before marking eligibility.

# Agency SEO

---

## Core Checks

Audit technical SEO, indexability, page architecture, keyword mapping, content depth, E-E-A-T, schema, backlinks, and conversion.

## Recommended Outputs

Create page map, content cluster, local/authority plan, conversion fixes, and 90-day roadmap.

## Warnings

Avoid generic content and fake guarantees. Use industry-specific trust proof.
