## content-quality-audit

# Content Quality and E-E-A-T Audit

This file defines how Beyond SEO evaluates content quality, topical authority, helpfulness, trust, and E-E-A-T.

Content quality is not “more words.” It is whether the page gives users and search systems the information, proof, and confidence needed to choose the business.

---

## 1. Purpose

The content audit answers:

```text
Is this content useful enough, specific enough, trustworthy enough, and complete enough to rank and convert?
```

---

## 2. Content Types to Audit

```text
Homepage
Service pages
Product pages
Course pages
Location pages
Blog/support articles
FAQ/help content
Author/expert pages
Case studies
Testimonials/reviews pages
Comparison pages
Cost/pricing pages
Certification/credential pages
```

---

## 3. Core Content Quality Criteria

Check every important page for:

```text
Specificity
Search intent match
Originality
Depth
Clarity
Expertise
Trust proof
Freshness
Local relevance if needed
Conversion support
Internal linking
AEO answer readiness
```

---

## 4. Helpful Content Questions

Ask:

```text
Does this page solve the user’s real question?
Does it explain what the user should do next?
Does it contain original insight or proof?
Does it feel written for humans or just search engines?
Does it answer objections before the CTA?
Does it have enough detail for the decision?
Is it better than competitor pages?
```

If answer is no, content needs improvement.

---

## 5. E-E-A-T Framework

E-E-A-T means:

```text
Experience
Expertise
Authoritativeness
Trustworthiness
```

### Experience

Check:

```text
Real-world examples
Case studies
Before/after proof where compliant
Screenshots
Project examples
Stories
Actual process details
```

### Expertise

Check:

```text
Expert credentials
Doctor/instructor/founder proof
Author/reviewer identity
Accurate explanations
No overclaims
```

### Authoritativeness

Check:

```text
Backlinks
Citations
Mentions
Memberships
Certifications
Media references
Recognizable proof
Topical depth
```

### Trustworthiness

Check:

```text
Clear contact information
Policies
Disclaimers
Authentic reviews
Transparent pricing/financing where useful
Realistic claims
Secure forms/payment
```

---

## 6. YMYL Content Rules

YMYL content requires extra care.

YMYL includes:

```text
Medical
Dental
Legal
Financial
Safety/security
Government/civic
High-stakes education/career claims
```

Required for YMYL pages:

```text
Expert/doctor/reviewer credentials
Reviewed-by or medically reviewed note where appropriate
Last updated/reviewed date
Clear disclaimers
Risks/limitations
No exaggerated claims
No fake outcomes
Sources/citations where needed
Clear contact/business info
```

For medical/dental service pages, include:

```text
Candidacy
Procedure/process
Risks
Recovery
Cost/financing
Alternatives
Doctor credentials
Reviews/proof
Appointment CTA
```

---

## 7. Thin Content Audit

Thin content is not just low word count.

Thin content means:

```text
Too little useful information for the intent.
```

Signs:

```text
Service page under 400-500 words in a competitive niche
No process explanation
No cost/price guidance where relevant
No FAQs
No proof/trust signals
No internal links
Generic text copied across services
No local relevance
No unique expert insight
Only sales copy, no useful detail
```

Fix:

```text
Expand with useful sections, not filler.
```

## 13. Content Scoring

Score each page out of 10:

```text
Intent match: 0-2
Depth/completeness: 0-2
Specificity/originality: 0-2
Trust/E-E-A-T: 0-2
Conversion/internal links: 0-2
```

Labels:

```text
8-10: Strong
6-7: Improve
4-5: Weak
0-3: Critical rebuild
```

```text
It can be understood by search engines and answer engines.
```


## conversion-seo-audit

# Conversion SEO Audit

This file defines how Beyond SEO audits whether organic traffic turns into real business outcomes.

SEO is not only rankings. If traffic does not convert into calls, queries, appointments, enrollments, purchases, or qualified leads, the SEO system is incomplete.

---

## 1. Purpose

Conversion SEO answers:

```text
If we bring the right visitor to this page, will they take the next step?
```

For lead-generation SEO, this is as important as rankings.

---

## 2. Conversion Goals

Identify the primary goal:

```text
Phone calls
WhatsApp clicks
Appointment bookings
Contact form submissions
Course enrollments
Purchases
Quote requests
Demo bookings
Newsletter signups
Download/lead magnet
Map directions
Google Business Profile calls
```

If the user gives a target like 500 queries/month, conversion audit becomes mandatory.

---

## 3. Inputs

Use:

```text
Website pages
GA4 conversion data
GSC landing pages
CRM/source data
GBP insights
Call tracking
Form tracking
Heatmap/session recordings if available
User-provided query/lead numbers
```

If data is not available, inspect visible conversion paths and label actual conversion performance as not verified.

---

## 4. Conversion Path Audit

Check:

```text
Homepage CTA
Service/product/course page CTA
Sticky mobile CTA
Contact page
Booking page
Lead form
Phone number visibility
WhatsApp link
Live chat/chatbot
Calendar booking
Checkout/enrollment flow
Thank-you page
Confirmation flow
Follow-up flow
```

Ask:

```text
Can a user act within 5 seconds?
Can a mobile user call/book easily?
Is the CTA specific to the page?
Is trust proof near the CTA?
Is there too much friction?
```

---

## 5. Page-Specific CTA Rules

Every money page should have a page-specific CTA.

Examples:

```text
Book Dental Implant Consultation
Request Emergency Dental Appointment
Join AI Automation Course
Get Free SEO Audit
Schedule AI Automation Demo
Download Course Curriculum
```

Avoid generic CTA:

```text
Submit
Click Here
Learn More
Contact Us
```

Generic CTAs are not always wrong, but they are usually weaker on high-intent pages.

---

## 6. Mobile Conversion Audit

Check:

```text
Phone number clickable
WhatsApp clickable
Sticky CTA
Form easy to use
Buttons visible above fold
No popup blocking CTA
Fast enough loading
No layout shift pushing buttons
No tiny text/buttons
Map/directions link if local
```

For local service businesses, mobile conversion is often the main money path.

---

## 7. Trust Near CTA

Check if CTAs are supported by:

```text
Reviews
Ratings
Testimonials
Case studies
Before/after proof where compliant
Certifications
Doctor/instructor/expert credentials
Years of experience
Guarantees only if legitimate
Privacy/security reassurance
Payment/financing info
```

Example:

Weak:

```text
Book Now
```

Strong:

```text
Book Dental Implant Consultation
4.9-star patient reviews • Financing available • Doctor-led treatment planning
```

---

## 8. Friction Audit

Check:

```text
Too many form fields
No clear next step
No response-time expectation
No pricing/financing guidance
Confusing navigation
Slow page
Broken form
No confirmation message
No thank-you page
No follow-up automation
Too many competing CTAs
```

Fix:

```text
Shorten forms
Add response expectation
Add WhatsApp/call backup
Use page-specific forms
Add thank-you tracking
Add follow-up workflow
```

---

## 9. Local Business Conversion

For local businesses, check:

```text
Phone above fold
Sticky call button
Google Maps/directions
Opening hours
Emergency/same-day wording where accurate
Appointment link

```

For dentists/doctors:

```text
Book consultation

```

---

## 10. Course/Academy Conversion

For course websites, check:

```text

Pricing/enrollment clarity
```

Weak:

```text
Enroll now
```

Strong:

```text

```

---

For ecommerce, check:

```text

Images

Availability

Payment options

Related products
Category filters

```

---

## 12. Query/Lead Growth Modeling

If user gives a target, calculate rough required traffic/interactions.

Formula:

```text
Required qualified visits/interactions = Target queries / Conversion rate
```

Examples:

```text
500 queries/month at 10% conversion = 5,000 qualified visits/interactions
500 queries/month at 7% conversion = 7,143 qualified visits/interactions
500 queries/month at 5% conversion = 10,000 qualified visits/interactions
```

Then split by channel:

```text
Organic service pages
Blog/support pages
Google Business Profile
Direct/brand
Referral/backlinks
Paid/retargeting if relevant
```

Do not claim exact future numbers. Use model assumptions.

---

## 13. Conversion Rate Assumption Rules

If no data exists, use cautious ranges and label as estimated.

Lead gen local services:

```text
5-15% blended interaction/query rate can be possible depending on intent, page quality, brand, and CTA strength.
```

Course websites:

```text
1-5% direct enrollment conversion may be realistic for cold organic traffic, higher for warm/brand traffic.
```

Do not guarantee.

Better:

```text
At a 10% estimated blended query rate, the site would need about 5,000 qualified visits/interactions. Actual rate must be verified with GA4, form, phone, WhatsApp, and GBP tracking.
```

---

## 14. Tracking Audit

Check whether conversions are measurable.

Required tracking:

```text
GA4
GSC
Form submit events
Phone click events
WhatsApp click events
Calendar booking events
Checkout/enrollment events
Thank-you pages
GBP UTM links
Call tracking if possible
CRM/source tracking
```

If not configured, mark as high priority.

Why:

```text
Without conversion tracking, SEO success becomes traffic theater.
```

## 16. Conversion Fix Priority

Use:

```text
Critical: form/phone/checkout broken or tracking impossible.
High: money pages lack clear CTA or trust proof.
Medium: CTA wording/layout improvements.
Low: polish/testing.
```

---

## 17. CRO + SEO Connection

Explain how conversion supports SEO goals:

```text
Better CTAs do not directly guarantee rankings, but they increase the value of traffic. When the goal is qualified queries, conversion improvements reduce the traffic volume needed to hit the target.
```

Example:

```text
If conversion improves from 5% to 10%, the site needs half the qualified traffic to reach the same query target.
```

---

## 18. Chatbot / Agent Conversion

If the site uses or wants a chatbot/AI agent, audit:

```text
Can it answer service/course questions?
Can it qualify leads?
Can it book appointments?
Can it hand off to WhatsApp/staff?
Does it avoid medical/legal overclaims?
Does it capture name/contact/intent?
Does it sync to CRM/sheet/email?
Is it visible but not annoying?
```

For sensitive niches:

```text
Chatbot must not replace professional advice.
Use disclaimers and handoff.
```

---

## 19. Common Mistakes

Do not:

```text
Focus only on rankings.
Ignore mobile CTAs.
Send all traffic to homepage.
Use same CTA on every page.
Ask for too many form fields.
Forget tracking.
Make medical/legal claims through chatbot.
Ignore GBP conversion.
Ignore WhatsApp/call clicks in Pakistan/local markets.
```

---

## 20. Final Conversion Gate

A page is conversion-ready when:

```text
The visitor understands the offer.
The next step is obvious.
Trust is visible.
Friction is low.
Mobile action is easy.
The conversion is trackable.
Follow-up exists.
```


## crawlability-indexing

# Crawlability and Indexing Audit

Checks whether search engines can discover, crawl, render, and index important pages.

---

## Checks

Sitemap, robots.txt, noindex, canonical, status codes, internal links, JS rendering, blocked resources, orphan pages.

## Critical Issues

Money pages blocked, noindexed, canonicalized away, broken, or missing from crawl path.

## Output

URL, issue, evidence, impact, fix, priority.


## eeat-ylym-audit

# E-E-A-T and YMYL Audit

Audits experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness, and high-stakes content requirements.

---

## YMYL Areas

Medical, dental, legal, financial, safety, government, health, and high-stakes career/education claims.

## Required Signals

Expert credentials, author/reviewer, last updated date, disclaimers, risk/limitations, proof, reviews, sources, and clear contact information.

## Output

Page, missing trust signal, risk, fix, priority.

This file defines the complete Beyond SEO audit process for full website audits, SEO + AEO/GEO reviews, client reports, proposal reports, and 30/60/90-day growth plans.

A full-site audit is not a checklist dump. It is a decision system that identifies what is blocking ranking, visibility, authority, conversion, and AI-search readiness.

```text
Full SEO audit
Website SEO audit
SEO + AEO audit
Technical + content audit
Client SEO report
90-day SEO plan
500-query growth plan
Website growth strategy
Competitor-based SEO plan
```

---

```text
What is stopping this website from ranking?
```

## specialist-checks

# Specialist evidence workflows

## Browser-rendered comparison
For JavaScript sites, capture the same URL's fetched HTML and rendered DOM using an available browser. Save timestamp, final URL, device, viewport, load/wait condition, screenshot and rendered DOM artifact. Compare title, primary heading, visible main content, canonical, robots, navigation and links. Record redirects, consent state and blocked resources. Browser rendering is not Google rendering or indexation. If browser access fails, report Not verified; never treat an empty HTML shell as proof of thin content.

Use `tools/site_checks.py` with saved page observations. Each page needs `url`, `links`, `priority`; optional `fetched_text`, `rendered_text`, `rendered_artifact`, `rendered_at`, `structured_data`. This is an offline review helper, not a browser crawler. Review candidates before converting them into finding-contract records.

## WordPress and WooCommerce
Sample products, variable products, categories, filtered/sorted pages, pagination, search, author/date archives and cart/checkout/account pages. Verify actual status, canonical, robots, sitemap inclusion, navigation and visible product data. Check conflicting SEO-plugin output in source and rendered DOM. Product variations may have legitimate unique URLs/content; filters may serve search demand. Never blanket noindex archives/filters or canonicalize variations without page-purpose and owner evidence. Confirm production vs staging before changing indexing controls.

## Internal links
Count unique referring pages within the observed sample, excluding external links and fragments. Flag owner-priority pages with zero observed inlinks as review candidates; only call a page orphaned after sufficiently complete discovery. Sample path variants and query parameters explicitly. Suggest links from topically relevant pages using natural descriptive anchors. Record crawl coverage and navigational context; raw inlink counts do not establish ranking impact.

## Migration safeguards
Before launch, inventory important old URLs and intended new destinations. Test HTTP status, full redirect chains, destination relevance, canonical, robots, staging noindex, XML sitemap, internal links and analytics continuity. After launch repeat against production, retain old/new evidence and compare first-party windows. Do not redirect all removed URLs to the homepage. Require a reviewed mapping and rollback plan for production edits.

## Structured data
Parse JSON-LD separately from eligibility assessment. Check types, required/recommended properties, visible-content consistency and current official documentation. Use Google's Rich Results Test where relevant and Schema.org validator for vocabulary; save dated results. JSON parsing alone cannot confirm validity, eligibility or appearance. Do not mark unsupported types as eligible or promise rich results.

## Prioritization and reliability
Use observed severity, affected scope, owner-designated business importance, effort and dependencies. Unknown effort/impact stays Not verified. Changes to redirects, canonical, noindex or deletion need page-purpose review and validation/rollback instructions. Prioritize evidence-backed actions; no invented traffic forecasts.

Keep configurable page/time/request budgets and stop reasons. Reuse saved artifacts for analysis, but distinguish reused collection time from fresh checks; resumable live crawling remains unavailable unless the selected crawler supports it. On partial failures retain successful observations and list failed/unattempted checks.

For maintenance run `tools/validate_skill.py`, regression tests and review `core/current-guidance-register.json`. Verify relevant current official guidance at use, including https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/search-gallery and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes. The tests contain synthetic evaluation cases, not proof of live-site accuracy. Expand with anonymized, authorized real-site cases and independently reviewed expected findings before claiming real-world coverage.

## 12.1 Spam-Policy Technical Risk Audit

Check whether site code, scripts, ad platforms, popups, redirects, or third-party libraries create search-quality risk.

Look for:


If back button hijacking is suspected, inspect browser behavior and scripts. Recommended fix:


## traffic-drop-diagnosis

# Traffic-Drop Diagnosis

Identify the metric/property: clicks, impressions, sessions, key events, qualified leads or revenue. Record onset, magnitude, windows, affected pages, country, device, search type and provenance. An analytics-session decline can be a measurement change even when GSC clicks are stable.

1. Validate tracking, exports, filters, consent, attribution, timezone and events. Check incomplete dates and equal-length windows.
2. Check deployments, outages, migrations, redirects, robots/noindex, canonicals, sitemaps, security/manual-action notices and URL Inspection. Compare affected URLs/templates rather than homepage status alone.
3. Segment brand/non-brand with query coverage limits, page type, country, device and supported appearance fields. Separate demand, click-through and position changes.
4. Review seasonality, prior-year demand, business changes and dated SERP samples. Trends and competitor changes support hypotheses, not causal proof.
5. Check the official [Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/) and [traffic-drop guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops). Update timing overlap is not proof of cause.

Output: hypothesis, supporting evidence IDs, contradicting evidence, unknowns, next discriminating check, confidence, owner and action. Prioritize verified access/indexing or measurement defects before speculative rewrites. Do not recommend mass deletion, disavowal or sitewide redirects based on correlation alone.

For multilingual/ecommerce/migrations, inspect hreflang/canonical interactions, product/category/faceted intent, URL mapping and redirect chains on representative templates. For server logs, verify bot identity and redact identifiers; a Googlebot user-agent alone is insufficient.
